把一段飲品原片做成多角度短片，可以先安排六個畫面任務，生成主片，再將難以完成的冰塊鏡頭獨立補做，最後剪回主片。這裡的六個角度是生成視角，不是現場六台攝影機的紀錄。

以下以本站飲品案例說明主片、補鏡與剪輯的分工，並提供兩份對應指令。模型、模式、片長與解析度是案例設定，不代表所有帳號目前都有相同選項。

## 三份素材各負責什麼？

先把表演與構圖分開指定，避免只寫「杯內仰拍」，卻沒有參考交代位置。

| 冰塊動作片 | 5 秒 | 專門交代落下與入水動作 |

先核對人物、杯把、瓶子與場景，再生成。原片沒有拍到的新角度仍需檢查，不把生成液體或配料當實際飲品品質證明。

## 準備三份素材

先把下面的檔案整理好。影片交代「怎麼動」，圖片交代「從哪裡看」。

## 先看懂六個鏡頭

![開瓶、桌面倒入、俯拍、杯內加冰、加入汽水及喝一口六個鏡頭。](https://chaozhibai.ai/article-covers/drink-six-shots.png)

*圖片提供細節參考。圖上秒數是截圖時間，不是每一鏡的長度。*

1. **0–2.5 秒｜開瓶** 放大手與瓶蓋，讓動作清楚。
2. **2.5–4 秒｜桌面倒入** 看瓶口與杯子的位置。
3. **4–5.5 秒｜上方俯拍** 看液體進入杯內。
4. **5.5–8 秒｜杯內仰拍** 從杯裡向上看冰塊落下。
5. **8–11 秒｜加入汽水** 看透明汽水、氣泡和液體顏色。
6. **11–15 秒｜喝一口** 回到人物，完成動作後短暫停留。

這裡的「六機位」是六種生成角度，不是現場架六台攝影機。秒數用來規劃節奏，最後仍需依生成結果剪輯。

## 主影片 Prompt｜六個角度

### 主影片 Prompt｜六個角度

~~~text

Use the reference video as the exact source performance. Soft even indoor daylight, warm cozy tone. Vertical 9:16. Multi-camera coverage of one continuous performance: same actions, same timing, same hands, hard cuts between camera angles on the action beats.

THE DRINK
The liquid from the black bottle is deep amber. The soda is completely clear and colorless, like Sprite. After the ice and soda are added, the drink becomes a clear, transparent, lighter sparkling amber with fizzing bubbles, like iced tea with soda. Never white, beige, milky, creamy or cloudy at any moment.

[00:00-02.50] Shot 1: Extreme close-up of her hands twisting the cap off the black bottle; the cap cracks open at 00:01.00. Macro lens, very shallow depth of field, the gold neck catching the light. Hard cut to Shot 2.
[02.50-04.00] Shot 2: Low angle at table height beside the tumbler, the deep amber liquid starts pouring from the black bottle into the tumbler. Hard cut to Shot 3.
[04.00-05.50] Shot 3: Top-down overhead looking straight into the tumbler, the deep amber stream pours from the black bottle's neck at the top of frame and splashes into the amber liquid, a ring of foamy bubbles forming on the surface, her white T-shirt soft beyond the rim. Hard cut to Shot 4.
[05.50-08.00] Shot 4: Macro slow motion from inside the tumbler looking up, her fingers drop an ice cube that plunges into the amber liquid exactly at 00:07.20, bubbles and ripples, her face blurred above the rim. Hard cut to Shot 5.
[08.00-11.00] Shot 5: Close-up at the rim of the tumbler from the side, crystal-clear Sprite-like soda pours from the green bottle over the ice, the deep amber lightens into a clear sparkling light amber, lively fizz and rising bubbles. Hard cut to Shot 6.
[11.00-15.00] Shot 6: Shot from behind the pink roses as a soft foreground, the camera slides sideways to reveal her lifting the tumbler and drinking, eyes closing, a satisfied relaxed expression. Finish the action by 14.50 seconds, then hold the final frame until 15.00 seconds.
~~~

## 冰塊鏡頭不好，就單獨補這一段

「補鏡」就是另外做一小段，換掉主影片中不理想的畫面。這裡繼續用 **Seedance 2.5（omni\_reference）1080p，生成 5 秒**。

@video1｜冰塊動作影片

控制冰塊怎麼落下。

@image1｜杯內仰拍構圖

控制觀看位置，讓鏡頭真的在杯子裡。

此段只做冰塊入水，不同時要求倒汽水或喝一口。先確認角度正確，再看冰塊、氣泡和手指細節。

## 補鏡 Prompt｜杯內仰拍冰塊

加入冰塊動作影片與構圖圖，將 @video1、@image1 換成實際素材標記。

~~~text
ACTIVE REFERENCES
@video1 is the 5-second ice-drop action reference. @image1 controls the inside-the-cup camera position and composition.

One single continuous shot, no cuts. Recreate exactly the camera position and composition of the reference image: a macro camera placed INSIDE the large cream-colored tumbler, looking straight up through the liquid toward the round steel rim opening. The lower two-thirds of the frame is deep amber liquid seen from within, backlit and glowing; the upper third is the circular rim opening, and through it the young woman from the reference video (long brown hair, blunt bangs, glasses pushed up on her head, white T-shirt) leans over and looks down into the cup, her face softly blurred.

Action, following the timing of the reference video: her fingers hold a clear ice cube above the rim, then drop it. At exactly 00:02.70 the ice cube plunges through the surface into the amber liquid in slow motion, a burst of bubbles and a swirling trail, the cube tumbling down toward the camera, ripples spreading across the underside of the surface. Afterwards small bubbles keep rising and the ice cube floats back up and bobs.

The liquid is clear, transparent, deep amber, like iced black tea. Never milky or cloudy. Soft even indoor daylight from above. Photoreal, shallow depth of field, crisp detail, vertical 9:16.

SOUND (ASMR, no music, no voice): quiet room tone, then at 00:02.70 a bright glassy clink and a deep muffled underwater plunk, followed by soft fizzing bubbles.

DO NOT show the outside of the cup. DO NOT cut to another angle. DO NOT add text, logos or other people.
~~~

## 把主影片與補鏡接起來

![原片剪短後先做480p探路，再做1080p正式版；另製作杯內補鏡，最後剪輯合成。](https://chaozhibai.ai/article-covers/drink-production-flow.png)

*上排做主影片，下排補冰塊鏡頭，最後合併。圖片提供細節參考。*

**保留開瓶與倒入 → 換入冰塊補鏡 → 接回汽水與喝一口。** 切點前後對齊杯口、手的高度、液體顏色和聲音。

不要直接照抄其他素材的截取秒數。先找到冰塊進入畫面、落水與氣泡的可用片段，再調整前後鏡頭長度。做原片／AI 子母畫面對照時，標清楚兩種畫面並對齊動作。

第一遍：看動作

開瓶、倒入、加冰、汽水、喝一口是否接得起來？

第二遍：看細節

人物、杯把、瓶子是否一致？飲料應保持透明琥珀色。

第三遍：聽聲音

金屬旋蓋不要變成開罐聲；冰塊和倒水聲要對上畫面。

模型與畫質選項，以 Higgsfield 畫面顯示為準。

## 主片有問題，什麼時候只補一鏡？

主題、前後動作與多數鏡頭已可用時，先處理局部。不必為杯內角度不理想，就反覆生成整支影片。

<div className="guide-table-scroll">

| 問題 | 先定位 | 可採取方向 |
|---|---|---|
| 杯內仰拍變成外部角度 | 構圖參考與鏡頭位置 | 單獨補鏡，先縮小任務 |
| 冰塊落水時間不合 | 動作參考與剪接點 | 依實際可用段落調整 |
| 液體顏色不同 | 原片、指令與相鄰鏡 | 核對同一色彩再替換 |
| 杯把方向跳動 | 前後手部與杯口 | 改切點、換結果或補真拍 |
| 開蓋聲像另一種容器 | 容器與音效內容 | 修正聲音，不以畫面通過取代聽檢 |

</div>

若整體人物、道具與順序都不符，先回到素材與主片任務，不能靠一個補鏡修好全部問題。
